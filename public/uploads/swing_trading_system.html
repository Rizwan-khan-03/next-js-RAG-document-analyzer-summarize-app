Complete swing trading system — step by step process, daily roadmap, Chartink prompt, and position size calculator

1
Market context check
Pehle bada picture dekho
Nifty 50 ka daily chart dekho — uptrend hai ya downtrend?
Nifty 50-day MA ke upar hai to market bullish — trades lo
Nifty 50-day MA ke neeche hai to sirf watchlist banao, trade mat lo
Budget, RBI policy, ya major event ka din hai to size chhoti rakho
2
Chartink scan chalao
3:15 PM ke baad run karo
Chartink.com pe apna saved scan run karo — "Prompt" tab mein formula diya hai
CSV download karo — sab results ek jagah
SME/BE stocks skip karo — liquidity kam hoti hai
Sirf woh stocks rakho jinka daily turnover ₹15 crore+ ho
3
Base quality check karo
Har stock ka chart manually dekho
Minimum 4-6 weeks ka tight sideways base hona chahiye
Base ke andar range 15-20% se zyada nahi honi chahiye
Agar stock already 20%+ upar bhaag chuka hai bina base ke — skip karo
Agar 52-week high pe bilkul hai — wait karo, pehla attempt reject hota hai
44-day MA rising hona chahiye ya flat — declining MA pe entry mat lo
4
Breakout confirm karo
Volume sabse zaroori hai
Breakout day volume average 20-day volume se 2x+ hona chahiye
Breakout candle strong honi chahiye — strong body, chhoti lower wick
Volume spike nahi hai to breakout fake ho sakta hai — wait karo
NSE pe volume check karo — BSE ka volume misleading ho sakta hai
5
Entry decide karo
2 approach hain — apna choose karo
Aggressive entry
Breakout din 10:30-11 AM ke baad — jab price hold kar rahi ho
9:15-10 AM mein mat lo — pehle ghante mein bahut noise hoti hai
Conservative entry (better risk:reward)
Breakout ke 1-3 din baad pullback wait karo breakout level tak
Pullback mein volume kam hona chahiye — buying interest intact hai
Wahan se bounce aaye to entry lo — better SL, better R:R
6
Position size nikalo
Calculator tab mein karo
Total capital ka sirf 1-2% risk per trade — kabhi nahi todna yeh rule
Shares = Max risk ÷ (Entry price - SL price)
Ek trade mein poora capital mat lagao — chahe setup kitna bhi perfect lage
Risk:Reward minimum 1:2 — target SL se 2x door hona chahiye
7
GTT set karo Zerodha pe
CNC — OCO order
Portfolio → Holdings → stock ke saamne GTT → OCO select karo
Upper trigger = Target price, Lower trigger = SL price
Dono pe quantity same rakho — quantity mismatch check karo
Product CNC hona chahiye — MIS (intraday) nahi
GTT set karne ke baad screenshot lo — confirm karo sab sahi hai
8
Trade manage karo
Yahan sabse zyada log galti karte hain
SL hit hone pe GTT mat hatao — yeh discipline ka asli test hai
Loss ke baad same din doosra trade mat lo — "revenge trading" hai yeh
Target 1 hit hone pe aadha position sell karo, baaki hold karo
Jab profit 1:1 ho jaye, SL breakeven (entry price) pe le aao
3 consecutive loss ke baad pause lo — kuch din trading band rakho
Golden rule

"SL set karo aur bhool jao — market ko kaam karne do. Screen ghoorte rehna decisions kharab karta hai."

8:00 – 9:00 AM
Pre-market prep
Kal ke trades ka status check karo. Nifty futures dekho. Koi bada news/event hai kya aaj? Agar result day ya RBI day hai to trading mat karo.
9:00 – 9:15 AM
Watchlist ready karo
Kal raat ke scan ke top 3-5 stocks open karo TradingView pe. Entry levels, SL, target — sab already decide hona chahiye. Market khulne ke baad decide karna galat hai.
9:15 – 10:00 AM
Sirf dekho — kuch mat karo
Pehle 45 minute bahut volatile hote hain — false moves common hain. Bas observe karo, note karo kahan price ja rahi hai.
10:00 – 11:00 AM
Entry window
Agar watchlist stock apne trigger level pe hai aur volume strong hai — entry lo. CNC mein, GTT immediately set karo entry ke baad.
11:00 AM – 2:30 PM
Screen se door raho
GTT set hai — kuch karne ki zaroorat nahi. Har ghante check karna anxiety deta hai aur galat decisions karwata hai. Kaam karo, life jiyo.
2:30 – 3:00 PM
Open trades ka ek baar review
Koi badi news toh nahi aayi? Position theek hai? Koi adjustment ki zaroorat nahi to mat karo.
3:15 – 3:30 PM
Closing candle dekho + scan chalao
Aaj ke trades ka closing candle kaisa bana? Kal ke liye Chartink scan chalao. Top candidates note karo. GTT/holdings update check karo.
After 3:30 PM
Journal likho — sirf 5 minute
Aaj kya kiya, kyon kiya, kya hua. Yeh sabse important habit hai — bina journal ke improvement nahi hoti.
Weekly review — har Sunday

Pichle hafte ke sab trades dekho. Kya pattern dikh raha hai losses mein? Kya working hai? Kya change karna hai? 30 minute weekly review = months of learning compressed.

Yeh formula Chartink.com pe paste karo → Create Scan → Edit, run and save scan. Har din 3:15 PM ke baad run karo jab market close ho jaaye.
( {cash} ( latest close > latest sma(latest close,44) ) and ( latest close > 1.02 * latest sma(latest close,20) ) and ( latest volume > 2 * latest sma(latest volume,20) ) and ( latest close > 1 day ago max(20,latest high) ) and ( weekly close[1] <= weekly close[2] * 1.08 ) and ( weekly close[2] <= weekly close[3] * 1.08 ) and ( weekly close[3] <= weekly close[4] * 1.08 ) and ( latest close > 30 ) and ( latest volume * latest close > 10000000 ) )
Yeh formula kya filter karta hai
44-day MA ke upar trade kar raha ho (uptrend)
20-day MA se 2%+ upar (fresh breakout zone)
Volume average se 2x+ zyada (genuine buying)
20-din ka high todha (actual breakout)
Pichle 3 weeks zyada nahi bhaaga (base quality)
Price ₹30+ aur turnover ₹1 Cr+ (liquidity filter)
Results aane ke baad kya karo
Yeh sirf shortlist hai — har stock ka chart manually check karo
Top 5 stocks ke charts Claude ko bhejo — entry/SL/target nikaalenge
Scan result dekh ke directly trade mat lo bina chart dekhe
Apna capital aur trade details daalo — kitne shares lene hain automatically nikaal dega.
